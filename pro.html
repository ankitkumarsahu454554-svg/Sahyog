The Cyclone Rescue Bottleneck

Why traditional uncoordinated messaging fails ground rescue workers.

Before: Chaotic Forwarded Messages

Delayed triage, blind dispatch, duplicate truck runs

  • Hundreds of forwards: Urgent medical requests get lost amid rumors and outdated information.
  • Unchecked duplication: Multiple volunteer squads deploy to the same location while nearby areas receive nothing.
  • Language barriers: Regional Telugu dialects or mixed phrases take precious minutes to translate manually.

After: SahyogAI Triage & Orchestration

Structured urgency, genuine similarity checks, tracked inventory

  • Bilingual Structured Triage: Extracts affected people, trapped status, and supplies needed.
  • Genuine Duplicate Search: Jaccard token analysis and geo-proximity detect potential duplicates across real records.
  • Atomic Dispatch & Tracking: Assignments and relief supplies lock in real-time, preventing double allocation.
